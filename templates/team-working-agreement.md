- Do you want to own it?
- Do you have the time, skills, access, and authority to deliver?
- What support or change is needed?
- Which existing work will stop or move if you take this on?

Suggested 25-minute agenda:

1. Review last week's commitments as done or not done, with evidence where useful. Five minutes.
2. Discuss the most consequential obstacles and decide how to address them. Fifteen minutes.
3. Confirm the next commitments, owners, and dates. Five minutes.

If an issue requires more time, assign a separate discussion and its owner. Do not let it disappear when the meeting ends.
